Our Legacy

Our Healing Legacy

V P G Ayurveda Hospital & Kalari Marma Chikithsalayam was established in 1998 by Dr. V. P. Gopalakrishnan, a renowned Ayurvedic practitioner with a vision to bring authentic traditional healing to the community of Karanthur, Kozhikode. What started as a small clinic has grown into a comprehensive healthcare center specializing in traditional Kerala Ayurvedic treatments.

Over the past 25+ years, we have remained committed to the ancient principles of Ayurveda while integrating modern comfort and safety standards. Our hospital has become a trusted name in authentic Ayurvedic healing, attracting patients from across Kerala and beyond.

Milestones

Our Journey

Follow our evolution from a small traditional clinic to a comprehensive Ayurvedic healthcare center serving the community for over two decades.

1998

Foundation

Dr. V. P. Gopalakrishnan established the hospital with a focus on traditional Kalari Marma therapy and basic Ayurvedic treatments, starting with just two treatment rooms.

2003

Expansion

Added Panchakarma treatment facilities and expanded to include specialized therapy rooms, herbal medicine preparation unit, and patient accommodation facilities.

2010

Innovation

Introduced modern diagnostic equipment while maintaining traditional treatment methods. Added yoga and meditation facilities for holistic healing approaches.

2018

Recognition

Received state-level recognition for excellence in traditional medicine and became a preferred training center for Ayurvedic students and practitioners.

2024

Present Day

A comprehensive healthcare facility offering 15+ specialized treatments, with a team of expert practitioners and modern facilities while staying true to traditional healing principles.

Our Philosophy

Our Philosophy

Rooted in ancient wisdom, our approach to healing encompasses the physical, mental, and spiritual aspects of wellness.

Holistic Healing

We believe in treating the whole person - body, mind, and spirit - rather than just symptoms. Our treatments address the root cause of ailments while promoting overall wellness.

Authentic Tradition

We maintain the purity of traditional Ayurvedic practices while ensuring safety and comfort. Our treatments follow ancient texts and time-tested methodologies.

Personalized Care

Every patient receives individualized treatment plans based on their unique constitution (Prakriti), health conditions, and specific needs.

Continuous Learning

We constantly update our knowledge and skills through research, training, and collaboration with other traditional medicine practitioners worldwide.

Community Service

We believe in giving back to the community through free health camps, awareness programs, and making traditional medicine accessible to all sections of society.

Ethical Practice

We maintain the highest ethical standards in all our interactions, treatments, and business practices, ensuring transparency and trust in every aspect of our service.
