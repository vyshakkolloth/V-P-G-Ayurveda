Authentic Kerala Ayurveda

V P G Ayurveda Hospital & Kalari Marma Chikithsalayam has been serving the community of Karanthur, Kozhikode with authentic traditional Ayurvedic treatments for over two decades. Our hospital combines ancient wisdom with modern comfort to provide holistic healing experiences.
