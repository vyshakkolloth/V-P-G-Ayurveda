# Project Outline: V P G Ayurveda Hospital Website

## Website Structure (4 Pages)

### 1. index.html - Homepage
**Purpose**: Create immediate impact and guide visitors to key actions
**Sections**:
- Navigation bar with logo and main menu
- Hero section with animated background and compelling messaging
- About V P G Ayurveda Hospital overview
- Featured treatments carousel (Kalari Marma, Panchakarma, Traditional therapies)
- Interactive treatment selector quiz
- Success stories testimonials
- Quick booking CTA section
- Footer with contact information

### 2. treatments.html - Treatments & Services
**Purpose**: Comprehensive showcase of all Ayurvedic treatments offered
**Sections**:
- Navigation bar
- Treatments hero section
- Kalari Marma Therapy detailed section with interactive body map
- Panchakarma treatments with program builder
- Traditional Kerala Ayurvedic therapies
- Specialized treatments for specific conditions
- Treatment packages and pricing
- Booking integration
- Footer

### 3. about.html - About Us & Facilities
**Purpose**: Build trust and showcase expertise and facilities
**Sections**:
- Navigation bar
- About hero section with hospital imagery
- Hospital history and legacy
- Doctor profiles and credentials
- Facilities tour (virtual gallery)
- Traditional vs modern approach explanation
- Awards and certifications
- Patient care philosophy
- Footer

### 4. contact.html - Contact & Booking
**Purpose**: Facilitate easy communication and appointment booking
**Sections**:
- Navigation bar
- Contact hero section
- Interactive contact form with treatment selection
- Doctor consultation scheduler
- Hospital location with embedded map
- Contact information and hours
- Emergency contact details
- Social media integration
- Newsletter signup
- Footer

## Key Interactive Components

### 1. Treatment Recommendation Quiz (Homepage)
- Multi-step questionnaire for personalized treatment suggestions
- Dynamic results with booking integration
- Progress tracking and results sharing

### 2. Interactive Kalari Marma Body Map (Treatments page)
- Clickable body diagram showing 108 Marma points
- Hover effects and detailed information panels
- Treatment recommendations based on selected points

### 3. Panchakarma Program Builder (Treatments page)
- Customizable program creation based on health goals
- Interactive calendar with daily activities
- Diet planning and progress tracking

### 4. Doctor Consultation Scheduler (Contact page)
- Real-time appointment booking system
- Doctor availability calendar
- Video consultation options
- Pre-consultation health assessment

## Content Strategy

### SEO-Optimized Content
- Keyword-rich treatment descriptions
- Local SEO for Karanthur, Kozhikode location
- Educational blog content about Ayurveda
- Patient testimonials and success stories

### Visual Content Requirements
- Hero images for each page
- Treatment process illustrations
- Doctor and facility photographs
- Traditional Ayurvedic imagery
- Before/after treatment visuals
- Ambiance and environment photos

### Technical Implementation
- Responsive design for all devices
- Fast loading times with optimized images
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1)
- SEO-friendly URL structure
- Schema markup for medical organization
- Local business listing integration
