# Design System for V P G Ayurveda Hospital Website

## Design Philosophy

### Core Aesthetic Vision
**Serene Authenticity**: A design language that embodies the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda while maintaining modern sophistication. The visual identity should evoke immediate trust, tranquility, and healing through carefully curated elements that speak to both traditional authenticity and contemporary wellness.

### Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- **Sacred Saffron**: #E67E22 (warm, spiritual, energizing)
- **Healing Green**: #27AE60 (natural, restorative, balanced)
- **Pure White**: #FFFFFF (cleanliness, purity, clarity)

**Secondary Colors:**
- **Earth Brown**: #8B4513 (grounding, stability, tradition)
- **Golden Turmeric**: #F39C12 (healing, warmth, vitality)
- **Deep Forest**: #1E4D2B (wisdom, depth, connection to nature)

**Accent Colors:**
- **Soft Cream**: #FDF2E9 (gentle, nurturing, peaceful)
- **Muted Gold**: #D4AF37 (luxury, authenticity, heritage)

### Typography
**Display Font**: Playfair Display (elegant serif for headings)
- Conveys tradition, wisdom, and premium quality
- Used for main headings, hero text, and brand elements

**Body Font**: Inter (clean sans-serif for readability)
- Modern, accessible, highly legible
- Used for body text, navigation, and UI elements

**Accent Font**: Devanagari MT (for Sanskrit terms)
- Authentic representation of Ayurvedic terminology
- Used sparingly for treatment names and mantras

### Visual Language
**Organic Shapes**: Flowing, natural forms inspired by leaves, water, and human body contours
**Sacred Geometry**: Subtle integration of traditional patterns and mandalas
**Breathing Space**: Generous white space to create calm, uncluttered experiences
**Natural Textures**: Subtle paper-like textures and organic patterns

## Visual Effects & Styling

### Used Libraries & Effects
1. **Anime.js**: Smooth, organic animations for interactive elements
2. **p5.js**: Generative background patterns inspired by nature
3. **ECharts.js**: Wellness data visualization with Ayurvedic color schemes
4. **Splide.js**: Elegant treatment gallery carousels
5. **Pixi.js**: Subtle particle effects for healing ambiance
6. **Matter.js**: Physics-based interactions for body map
7. **Shader-park**: Ethereal background effects

### Animation & Motion
**Scroll Animations**: Gentle fade-ins and subtle parallax effects
**Hover States**: Soft glow effects and gentle scaling
**Loading States**: Organic breathing animations
**Transitions**: Smooth, natural easing curves

### Header & Hero Effects
**Background**: Animated aurora gradient flow in healing colors
**Text Treatment**: Gradient color cycling from saffron to green
**Interactive Elements**: Subtle shadow expansion and glow effects

### Interactive Components Styling
**Treatment Quiz**: Card-based interface with soft shadows and gentle hover lifts
**Body Map**: Clean, medical illustration style with interactive hotspots
**Booking System**: Calendar with organic shapes and warm color highlights
**Program Builder**: Step-by-step wizard with progress indicators

### Responsive Design
**Mobile-First**: Touch-friendly interactions with generous tap targets
**Tablet**: Optimized layouts for both portrait and landscape orientations
**Desktop**: Rich, immersive experiences with full interactive capabilities

### Accessibility Considerations
**Color Contrast**: Minimum 4.5:1 ratio for all text elements
**Focus States**: Clear, visible focus indicators for keyboard navigation
**Alternative Text**: Comprehensive descriptions for all visual elements
**Screen Reader**: Semantic HTML structure with proper ARIA labels
