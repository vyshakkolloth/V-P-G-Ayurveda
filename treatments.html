5-Action Detox

Panchakarma Detox Therapy

Panchakarma, meaning "five actions" in Sanskrit, is the ultimate Ayurvedic detoxification and rejuvenation therapy. This comprehensive treatment cleanses the body of accumulated toxins (Ama) and restores the natural balance of doshas through five specialized procedures.

1

Vamana (Therapeutic Vomiting)

Removes excess Kapha dosha through controlled emesis, treating respiratory conditions and congestion.

2

Virechana (Purgation)

Cleanses Pitta-related toxins through controlled bowel movements, improving liver function and skin health.

3

Basti (Medicated Enema)

Balances Vata dosha through herbal oil or decoction enemas, treating digestive and nervous system disorders.

4

Nasya (Nasal Administration)

Clears head and neck region through medicated nasal drops, treating sinusitis, headaches, and mental clarity.

5

Raktamokshana (Blood Letting)

Purifies blood through controlled extraction, treating skin disorders and blood-related conditions.

Benefits of Panchakarma:

Deep detoxification
Improved digestion
Better sleep quality
Enhanced immunity
Mental clarity

Time-Honored Remedies

Traditional Kerala Therapies

Experience authentic Kerala Ayurvedic treatments that have been practiced for centuries, each designed for specific healing purposes and overall wellness.

Abhyanga

Full body oil massage using warm medicated oils tailored to your body constitution. This rejuvenating therapy improves circulation, relieves muscle tension, and promotes deep relaxation.

Duration: 60 minutes ₹1,800
Benefits: Stress relief, improved skin health, better sleep, enhanced circulation

Shirodhara

Continuous pouring of warm herbal oil on the forehead, specifically targeting the third eye area. This deeply relaxing treatment calms the nervous system and promotes mental clarity.

Duration: 45 minutes ₹2,200
Benefits: Mental clarity, stress reduction, headache relief, improved concentration

Njavarakizhi

Unique Kerala therapy using rice boluses filled with medicinal herbs and warm milk. The heated bundles are massaged over the body, providing deep nourishment and strength.

Duration: 75 minutes ₹2,800
Benefits: Muscle strengthening, joint nourishment, skin rejuvenation, vitality boost

Udvartana

Herbal powder massage using coarse medicinal powders to create friction and heat. This stimulating treatment helps in weight management and cellulite reduction.

Duration: 45 minutes ₹1,600
Benefits: Weight loss, improved metabolism, cellulite reduction, skin toning

Pizhichil

Luxurious therapy involving continuous pouring of warm medicated oil over the body while being massaged simultaneously. Known as the "King's Treatment" for its royal origins.

Duration: 90 minutes ₹3,500
Benefits: Deep relaxation, nervous system balance, muscle tension relief, rejuvenation

Thalapothichil

Specialized head treatment where medicated herbal paste is applied to the scalp and covered with banana leaves. Excellent for neurological and psychological conditions.

Duration: 60 minutes ₹2,000
Benefits: Headache relief, mental calmness, hair health, neurological support
